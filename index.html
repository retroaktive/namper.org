Real amps, captured.

CHAIN · RIG BUILDER

A pedalboard that routes like a patchbay.

  • Drag-and-drop signal chain with parallel branches
  • Up to four independent rigs — guitar and vocals at once, each on its own input and output
  • Five EQ models — Tone, Para, Passive, Console and Bax — and a master section with saturation, limiter and LUFS metering
  • Presets on MIDI program-change slots, sharable as files

PLUGINS · AUv3 BOTH WAYS

Your other plugins. And NAMper inside theirs.

  • Add any Audio Unit effect you own straight from the blocks palette
  • The plugin’s own interface opens right inside the block editor
  • NAMper is an AUv3 effect too — load it in GarageBand, AUM or Logic
  • Plugin settings travel with your presets
LOOP · 4 TRACKS

A looper that keeps time for you.

  • The first loop sets the cycle — everything else lands on the bar grid
  • Circular pads draw the take as you play it, with a beam running the cycle
  • AUTO arms the next track, starts on your first note and snaps the loop to the bars you actually played
  • Loop editor: drag the edges, ÷2 or ×2, nudge the phase, pan, undo
  • Record post-FX, a raw input, another rig or the sampler — with tempo detection and a phase-locked click
SAMPLE · 16 PADS

Sixteen pads, four factory kits.

  • Load files or record straight into a pad — trim, pitch, loop, envelope
  • Factory kits: digital, analog and acoustic drums, plus FX & textures
  • Trigger quantize pulls your hits onto the grid while you record
  • MIDI notes 36–51 play the pads; kits save and recall as presets
PERFORM · SETLIST + LYRICS

Backing tracks, lyrics, and the whole show.

  • Setlists of backing tracks, with automatic tempo and key detection
  • Lyrics scroll on the glass — hold a line to pin it to the moment
  • Preset markers on the song timeline, snapped to bars
  • Multitrack recording — mix, dry input and backing — exported as a DAWproject
CONTROL · MIDI + TRIGGERS

Hands on the strings, not the screen.

  • Bluetooth MIDI pairing built in; MIDI learn for any CC or note
  • Teach it a sound — a knock on the body, a string slap — and map it to anything
  • Chromatic and polyphonic (strum) tuner
  • Multichannel audio interfaces, per-channel trim and naming
  • A feedback guard mutes the output before the mic finds the phone speaker
